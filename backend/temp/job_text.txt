Job Description:
Job Description
Role Purpose
The purpose of this role is to support basic development activities by assisting in coding and unit testing under close guidance from team members.
͏
Areas of responsibility
Coding and Configuration-Assist in basic coding under close guidance from Developer.
͏
Testing and Trial Runs-Assist in unit testing for assigned tasks, validate expected results, and record outcomes as per defined formats.
͏
Maintain Documentation-Document coding details, and test results for assigned tasks.
͏
Stakeholder Collaboration-Coordinate with developers to understand project goals, clarify basic queries, and provide updates on assigned work.
Mandatory Skills: AI/ML(RPA).
